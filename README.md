# The Foundry — Marketing & Growth Repository

Marketing operations repo for **The Foundry**, an event venue in Oswego County (Central New York), managed by **Alberts Group**. This repo is the working home for SEO strategy, ad campaign assets, performance reporting, brand guidelines that keep every channel pointed the same direction, and anything else related to marketing operations.

## Campaign goals, August–December

Every month runs the same three lanes — **SEO**, **Engagement**, and **Ads** — layered on top of the standing offer (the $5,000 winter wedding deal doesn't change month to month, so momentum has to come from narrative variation, seasonal keyword shifts, and progressive urgency, not repeated messaging).

| Month | SEO | Engagement | Ads |

| **August** | 2027–2028 booking push | $5K winter deal · 2027–28 booking · Fall Fest 9/26/26 | $5K winter deal · 2027–28 booking · Fall Fest 9/26/26 |
| **September** | 2027–2028 booking push | $5K winter deal · 2027–28 booking · Fall Fest 9/26/26 | $5K winter deal · 2027–28 booking · Fall Fest 9/26/26 |
| **October** | 2027–2028 booking push | $5K winter deal · 2027–28 booking · Holiday Market 12/6/26 | $5K winter deal · 2027–28 booking · Holiday Market 12/6/26 |
| **November** | 2027–2028 booking push | $5K winter deal · 2027–28 booking · Holiday Market 12/6/26 | $5K winter deal · 2027–28 booking · Holiday Market 12/6/26 |
| **December** | 2027–2028 booking push | $5K winter deal · 2027–28 booking | $5K winter deal · 2027–28 booking |

Full monthly detail (on-page/blog content, GBP updates, keyword targets, technical tasks, off-page opportunities) lives in `/seo/roadmap.md` and each month's brief in `/campaigns/`.

## Brand voice

All copy — SEO, ads, email, social — should be checked against `BRANDGUIDE.md` before publishing. Ad tone has landed on **confident but warm** after testing aspirational, urgent, and warm/direct registers.

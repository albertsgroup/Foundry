## Trend research summary

- **Lens-wipe reveal** — a hand swipes across the lens; empty space becomes the styled shot underneath. Currently the cleanest venue/location reveal format on Reels. ([Later, Instagram Reels Trends 2026](https://later.com/blog/instagram-reels-trends/))
- **Black-and-white-to-color reveal** — B&W opens, color floods in on the payoff beat. Built for transformation content (empty room → styled reception). ([Later, Instagram Reels Trends 2026](https://later.com/blog/instagram-reels-trends/))
- **Documentary-style storytelling** — candid, real interactions over posed shots; layered audio, natural flow. Dominant videography trend for 2026 weddings. ([THE WED, 14 Wedding Content Trends Taking Over 2026](https://thewed.com/magazine/wedding-content-trends-taking-over-2026))
- **Bridal/detail-as-main-character** — close-up texture shots (beading, place settings, handwriting) treated as a visual narrative, not filler. ([THE WED](https://thewed.com/magazine/wedding-content-trends-taking-over-2026))
- **"Book your date" carousel** — ends on a direct, low-pressure CTA slide ("still have Saturdays open — DM TOUR"). Ties to the Oct–Feb booking clustering window. ([SocialMon, Wedding Venue Instagram Post Ideas](https://www.socialmon.ai/blog/68-wedding-venue-instagram-post-ideas-with-examples-that-book-tours))
- **Wedding-guest era / Old Hollywood glam** — draped fabrics, pearl details, sparkly clutches; guest-facing style content trending into fall 2026. ([Who What Wear, Wedding-Guest Dressing Trends 2026](https://www.whowhatwear.com/fashion/trends/best-wedding-guest-outfit-trends-2026); example creator: [@theultimatebridestl](https://www.tiktok.com/@theultimatebridestl/video/7338169030240455979))
- **Real venue reference accounts (Upstate NY, for visual benchmarking):** [@thegroveofbv](https://www.instagram.com/thegroveofbv/) (The Grove of Butternut Valley, Morris NY) and this reel from a comparable upstate barn/resort venue: [instagram.com/reel/C6HBSYIriCt](https://www.instagram.com/reel/C6HBSYIriCt/). Curation account for trending wedding-reel styles generally: [@weddingreel](https://www.instagram.com/weddingreel/).

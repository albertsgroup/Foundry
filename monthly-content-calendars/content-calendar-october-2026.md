## Trend research summary (searched 2026-08-17, prioritizing sources from the last 30 days)

- **Peak wedding month** — October is projected to be the single most popular wedding month of 2026 nationally (76% of 2026 weddings fall May–October). Strong hook for a booking-forward month. ([Zola, 2026 First Look Report](https://www.zola.com/expert-advice/the-first-look-report-2026))
- **Jewel tones, velvet, candlelight** — emerald, sapphire, and ruby read as "expensive" instantly; velvet linens/runners paired with taper and pillar candles are the defining 2026 fall/winter decor formula, replacing LED-lit setups. ([The Knot, 2026 Wedding Decor Trends](https://www.theknot.com/content/wedding-decor-trends); real live posts: [instagram.com/p/DQ2dKm7DAIq](https://www.instagram.com/p/DQ2dKm7DAIq/), [instagram.com/reel/DVHA2fTE507](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DVHA2fTE507/))
- **"Get ready with me" / mirror moment** — couples and guests are documenting the full getting-ready block (makeup touch-ups, styling, the mirror moment) as its own emotionally rich content beat, not just prep footage before the "real" video starts. ([THE WED, Major Wedding Photography & Videography Trends for 2026](https://thewed.com/magazine/major-wedding-photography-videography-trends-for-2026))
- **Low-production, single-take content outperforming polish** — August 2026 Instagram trend data shows authenticity beating over-produced posts as AI-generated content floods feeds; single-take, minimally-edited clips are outperforming heavily cut ones. ([SocialBee, The Latest Instagram Trends, Aug 13 2026](https://socialbee.com/blog/instagram-trends/))
- **Cozy, home-inspired lighting** — 2026 weddings are shifting toward feeling like an intimate dinner party at home: warm accent lighting, sensory styling (flower bars, produce-based centerpieces) guests can see and smell, not just photograph. ([Pinterest Newsroom, Wedding Trend Report 2026](https://newsroom.pinterest.com/news/wedding-trend-report-2026/))
- **Holiday proposal season lead-in** — roughly 40% of proposals happen between late November and mid-February, with December the single most popular proposal month; engagement-photo search demand itself peaks in November. October is the natural month to plant that invitation. ([Sateur, 45 Proposal Ideas for 2026](https://sateur.com/blogs/proposals/proposal-ideas))
- **2026 fall color language** — plum, terracotta, sage, deep forest green, burgundy, espresso, merlot, antique gold. Useful sensory vocabulary for captions and shot notes this month. ([Joy, Fall Wedding Colors 2026](https://withjoy.com/blog/fall-wedding-colors-2026-10-palettes-with-swatches-flowers-styling-tips/))
- **Local reference accounts (Central NY, for visual/pacing benchmarking):** [@sky_armory](https://www.instagram.com/sky_armory/) (SKY Armory, downtown Syracuse) and [@hotelsyracuseweddings](https://www.instagram.com/hotelsyracuseweddings/) (Hotel Syracuse) — same accounts referenced in September's calendar, still the closest local comps for pacing.

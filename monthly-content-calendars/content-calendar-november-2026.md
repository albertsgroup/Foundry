## Trend research summary

- **Micro-detail hook** — open on one small texture (place card, handwriting, candle) before the wide shot. ([BlitzCut, Viral Wedding Reel Hooks 2026](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/wedding-reel-hooks-2026))
- **Unrepeatable-moment hook** — "the five minutes before" framing. ([BlitzCut](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/wedding-reel-hooks-2026))
- **Timeline / regret-prevention hooks** — practical booking-oriented Reels ("what to book first"). ([BlitzCut](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/wedding-reel-hooks-2026))
- **Transformation beat** — empty room to styled room on a single cut. ([BlitzCut](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/wedding-reel-hooks-2026))
- **"Before the aisle walk" unscripted moments** — quiet, candid, addressed-to-camera clips; a 2026 TikTok wedding trend. ([Wedded Wonderland, TikTok Wedding Trends 2026](https://weddedwonderland.com/tiktok-wedding-trends-2026))
- **Behind-the-scenes setup / "Instagram vs. reality"** and **Engagement Season Hype (Nov–Feb)** — venue post ideas. ([Scott Social Marketing, 14 Venue Post Ideas](https://www.scottsocialmarketing.com/blog/14-wedding-venue-social-media-post-ideas))
- **Stories for time-sensitive updates; location-tag every Reel** (wedding search is regional). ([Tripleseat](https://tripleseat.com/blog/how-to-use-instagram-to-promote-your-event-venue/); [BlitzCut](https://blitzcutai.com/blog/wedding-reel-hooks-2026))
- **Caveat on audio:** guides advise against trending sounds whose lyrics clash with the emotional beat. Prefer cinematic/emotional instrumental (on-brand).
